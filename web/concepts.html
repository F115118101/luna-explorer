Tokenization — cutting text into tokens分詞——把文字切成 token

The model never sees letters, only integer ids of text pieces模型從來看不到字母，只看到文字片段的整數編號

Before anything else, a tokenizer cuts the text into pieces called tokens and gives each piece an integer id. Today's standard is subword-level cutting: keep common words whole, and split rare or long words into reusable pieces such as roots, prefixes and suffixes — so unfriendly might become un · friend · ly. This keeps the vocabulary small and finite while still handling words never seen in training, by falling back to smaller known pieces (down to single bytes if needed).在一切開始之前，分詞器先把文字切成一段一段叫 token 的片段，每段給一個整數編號。現在的主流是子詞級切法：常見的詞保持完整，罕見或很長的詞拆成可重複使用的片段，例如字根、字首、字尾——所以 unfriendly 可能變成 un · friend · ly。這讓詞彙表維持小而固定的大小，又能處理訓練時沒看過的詞：往下退回更小、已知的片段（必要時退到單一位元組）。

Three common subword algorithms: BPE (start from single characters or bytes, repeatedly merge the most frequent adjacent pair; GPT-2 uses a byte-level version), WordPiece (BERT; picks merges by a language-model score and marks continuations with ##), and SentencePiece (Llama 1/2; treats the space as an ordinary symbol, so it needs no language-specific preprocessing and suits Chinese and Japanese).三種常見的子詞演算法：BPE（從單一字元或位元組開始，反覆合併最常相鄰的一對；GPT-2 用位元組版）、WordPiece（BERT；用語言模型分數決定合併，非字首的片段加 ##）、SentencePiece（Llama 1/2；把空白當成一般符號，不需語言專屬的前處理，對中文、日文友善）。

When does BPE stop merging? Training fixes a target vocabulary size in advance (GPT-2: 50,257); merging repeats, largest pair first, until the vocabulary reaches that size — not until the text is “fully” merged. A small or repetitive corpus can also run out of pairs that occur more than once before reaching that size, in which case merging stops early (this is what the toy corpus below does, since it is tiny). At inference the tokenizer no longer counts anything — it just applies the merges it learned, in the order it learned them.BPE 什麼時候停止合併？訓練前會先定好目標詞彙表大小（GPT-2 是 50,257）；合併一直挑最高次數的那一對繼續做，直到詞彙表到達這個大小為止——不是切到「切不動」才停。語料如果很小或重複性高，也可能在到達目標大小之前，就找不到出現兩次以上的組合，於是提早停止（下面的玩具語料因為太小，就是這種情況）。推論時分詞器不再計數，只是照著訓練時學到的合併順序，依序套用。

Token embeddingToken 嵌入向量

A token is just an id until it becomes a vector; similar meanings end up close togethertoken 一開始只是編號，變成向量後，意思相近的會靠在一起

The tokenizer cuts text into pieces (how it cuts); an embedding table turns each piece into a list of numbers (4,096 for Llama-7b). Training pushes words used in similar contexts close together. Position is added separately, because attention alone cannot tell word order.分詞器把文字切成片段（它怎麼切）；嵌入表把每個片段換成一串數字（Llama-7b 是 4,096 個）。訓練會把出現在相似語境的詞推近。位置資訊另外加進去，因為光靠注意力分不出字的順序。

Self-attention自注意力

Each token decides how much to look at every other token每個 token 決定要多關注其他每一個 token

For every token, attention scores how relevant each other token is, turns the scores into weights with softmax, and mixes the other tokens' vectors with those weights. In A baby sat on the chair, sat looks at baby and chair more than at the. A decoder-only model adds a causal mask: a token may only look at itself and earlier tokens.對每個 token，注意力先為其他每個 token 的相關程度打分，再用 softmax 把分數變成權重，最後用權重混合其他 token 的向量。在 A baby sat on the chair 裡，sat 對 baby 和 chair 的關注高於 the。decoder-only 模型多一個因果遮罩：token 只能看自己和之前的 token。

Next-token sampling下一個字怎麼選

Scores → softmax → probabilities → pick one → append → repeat分數 → softmax → 機率 → 挑一個 → 接上去 → 重複

The last layer gives one score (logit) per vocabulary word. Softmax turns them into probabilities that sum to 1. Temperature sharpens or flattens them, top-k keeps only the k most likely. Because each pick becomes part of the next input, an answer is a path — the reason LUNA models whole trajectories rather than single outputs. The entropy of the distribution says how unsure the model is.最後一層為詞彙表中每個字給一個分數（logit）。softmax 把它們變成加總為 1 的機率。溫度讓分布更尖或更平，top-k 只保留最可能的 k 個。每次挑出的字都成為下一步輸入的一部分，所以答案是一條路徑——這就是 LUNA 要建模整條軌跡、而不是單一輸出的原因。分布的熵代表模型有多不確定。

Probing activations: SAPLMA and ITI探測隱藏激活：SAPLMA 與 ITI

The two related works the paper compares LUNA with論文拿來和 LUNA 比較的兩個相關工作

Both methods look inside the LLM, like LUNA, but for a narrower question: is this statement true? Probing trains a small classifier on the hidden activations: if truthful and false statements sit in different places, a simple boundary can tell them apart. SAPLMA (Statement Accuracy Prediction based on Language Model Activations) does exactly that, and judges a statement once the output is finished. ITI (Inference-Time Intervention) goes one step further: it finds a “truthful direction” and nudges the activations along it while the model is generating. LUNA differs in scope (several trust aspects, not just true/false QA) and in timing: it can monitor every token as it is written.這兩個方法和 LUNA 一樣都往 LLM 內部看，但只回答比較窄的問題：這句話是真的嗎？探測（probing）是在隱藏激活上訓練一個小分類器：如果真話和假話落在不同的地方，一條簡單的邊界就能分開它們。SAPLMA（Statement Accuracy Prediction based on Language Model Activations）做的就是這件事，在輸出完成後判斷一句話。ITI（Inference-Time Intervention）更進一步：找出一個「真實方向」，在模型生成的同時把激活沿著它推。LUNA 的不同在範圍（多個可信賴性面向，不只是真假 QA）和時機：它可以在每個 token 寫出時就監控。

K-Means — hard groupingK-Means——硬分群

Each cluster becomes one abstract state每一群就是一個抽象狀態

K-Means alternates two steps: assign every point to its nearest centre, then move each centre to the mean of its points. It stops when nothing changes. States only appear where data exists, which is why clustering gives LUNA compact models. Try the ring data: K-Means cuts it into wedges, because it only knows round clusters.K-Means 反覆做兩步：指派——每個點歸給最近的中心；移動——每個中心移到自己那群點的平均。直到沒有變化為止。狀態只在有資料的地方出現，這就是分群讓 LUNA 的模型很精簡的原因。試試環形資料：K-Means 會把它切成扇形，因為它只認得圓形的群。

GMM — soft groupingGMM——軟分群

Gaussian Mixture Model, fitted with the EM algorithm高斯混合模型，用 EM 演算法擬合

A GMM describes the data as a mix of bell-shaped blobs, each with its own centre, size and tilt. EM alternates E-step (every point gets a probability of belonging to each blob) and M-step (re-fit each blob using those probabilities). Unlike K-Means, a point between two blobs belongs partly to both. Markov chain and stationary distribution馬可夫鏈與穩態分布

What a DTMC is, and what “long-run behaviour” meansDTMC 是什麼，以及「長期行為」指的是什麼

A discrete-time Markov chain hops between a few states; the chance of the next state depends only on the current one, and each row of the transition matrix sums to 1. LUNA's abstract model is such a chain whose states are the clusters from the PCA, K-Means and GMM pages. Let it wander: the share of time spent in each state settles on the stationary distribution π, which LUNA uses to weigh the states (SDE) and to find traps (SS, sink states).離散時間馬可夫鏈在幾個狀態之間跳動；下一個狀態的機率只取決於目前狀態，轉移矩陣每一列加總為 1。LUNA 的抽象模型就是這樣一條鏈，狀態就是PCA、K-Means、GMM 那幾頁的分群。讓它走一走：停留在各狀態的時間比例會穩定到穩態分布 π，LUNA 用它來替各狀態加權（SDE）和找出陷阱（SS，匯點狀態）。

Hidden Markov model and Baum-Welch隱馬可夫模型與 Baum-Welch

When the states you care about are hidden and you only see their symptoms真正的狀態看不到，只看得到它們的外在表現

Sometimes the states that really drive a sequence are not directly visible — you only see a symbol each one tends to produce. A hidden Markov model (HMM) models exactly that: a hidden chain of states, and a separate visible sequence of symbols it emits. It has three parts:有時候真正在驅動一串資料的狀態不是直接看得到的，你只看得到每個狀態傾向產生的符號。隱馬可夫模型（HMM）就是在描述這件事：一條看不見的隱藏狀態鏈，加上它發射出來、看得到的一串符號。它由三部分組成：

  • Hidden states that switch according to transition probabilities.隱藏狀態：依轉移機率互相切換。
  • Emission probabilities: each hidden state produces each visible symbol with its own probability.發射機率：每個隱藏狀態各自以一組機率產生看得到的符號。
  • Initial probabilities: how likely each hidden state is at the start.初始機率：一開始各隱藏狀態的可能性。

Once you have these numbers you can do two things with a new sequence of symbols. The forward algorithm tells you how likely the sequence is under the model. The Viterbi algorithm guesses the most likely hidden-state sequence behind it.有了這些數字，面對一串新符號可以做兩件事：前向算法算出這串符號在模型下有多可能；Viterbi 猜出背後最可能的隱藏狀態序列。

But where do the numbers come from? Usually you only have the visible sequences. That is a chicken-and-egg problem: with the hidden states you could just count, and with the numbers you could guess the hidden states. Baum-Welch (an EM algorithm) solves it by alternating: guess the hidden states “softly” with the current numbers (a 70% / 30% guess, not a hard choice), recount using those guesses, and repeat. Each round makes the training sequences at least as likely as before.那這些數字從哪來？通常你手上只有看得到的序列。這是雞生蛋、蛋生雞：知道隱藏狀態就能直接數出機率，知道機率又能猜出隱藏狀態。Baum-Welch（一種 EM 演算法）用輪流修正來解：先用目前的數字「軟性」猜隱藏狀態（例如 70% 是這個、30% 是那個，不硬選），再用這些猜測重新計數，然後重複。每一輪都會讓訓練序列的似然上升或不變。

The 8 training sequences (10 symbols each) were generated by a hidden “true” model. Press “1 EM round” and watch the learned numbers move toward it and the likelihood climb. The sequence you build with the a / b / c buttons (up to 8 symbols) is the one being decoded.下面的玩具圖用 4 個隱藏狀態（S1–S4，跟上面馬可夫鏈玩具的狀態數一樣）、3 種看得到的符號（a、b、c）。8 條訓練序列（各 10 個符號）由一個隱藏的「真實」模型產生。按「1 輪 EM」，看學到的數字怎麼靠近它、似然怎麼上升。你用 a / b / c 按鈕組出的序列（最多 8 個符號）就是被解碼的那一條。

Out-of-distribution detection分布外偵測

“Have I seen anything like this before?”, as a density and a threshold「我以前見過這種東西嗎？」，用密度與門檻來回答

A model is only trustworthy on inputs that resemble its training data. The textbook test (the paper's Eq. 2) estimates the density pX of the training inputs and flags anything below a threshold λ as out-of-distribution. The paper builds OOD inputs by rewriting SST-2 sentences in Shakespeare's style. In real LLMs the input space is far too large for a density estimate, so LUNA scores a path by its transition probabilities instead.模型只有在輸入像訓練資料時才可信。教科書上的做法（論文的式 2）是估計訓練輸入的密度 pX，低於門檻 λ 的就判為分布外。論文把 SST-2 的句子改寫成莎士比亞風格來製造分布外輸入。真實 LLM 的輸入空間太大，沒辦法估計密度，所以 LUNA 改用一條路徑的轉移機率來打分。

KL divergence and perplexityKL 散度與困惑度

Two ways to measure “how different” or “how surprised”衡量「差多少」與「多意外」的兩種方式

KL divergence measures how far a distribution Q is from a reference P. It is 0 only when they are identical and it is not symmetric. Perplexity measures how surprised a language model is by a text: the average number of words it was torn between. LUNA's PERP metric is the KL divergence between the perplexities of normal and abnormal data, and its SL metric is the KL divergence between a posterior and a prior.KL 散度衡量分布 Q 離基準 P 有多遠。只有兩者完全相同時才是 0，而且不對稱。困惑度衡量語言模型看到一段文字有多意外：平均在幾個字之間猶豫。LUNA 的 PERP 指標是正常與異常資料的困惑度之間的 KL 散度，SL 指標則是後驗與先驗之間的 KL 散度。
