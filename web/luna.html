LUNA

A model-based universal analysis framework for large language models: compress a decoder's hidden states into a small probabilistic state machine, bind trustworthiness semantics to it, and use it to catch hallucinations, out-of-distribution inputs and adversarial attacks. 一個以模型為基礎的 LLM 通用分析框架：把解碼器的隱藏狀態壓縮成小型機率狀態機，再綁定可信賴性語意，用來抓出幻覺、分布外（OOD）輸入與對抗攻擊。

01

Scale and opacity規模與黑箱

One Llama-7b token carries 32 decoder blocks × 4,096 dimensions = 131,072 numbers. Nobody can inspect that directly.Llama-7b 的單一 token 有 32 個解碼器區塊 × 4,096 維 = 131,072 個數字，無法直接檢視。

02

Generation is a stateful stochastic process生成是有狀態的隨機過程

Each token depends on every previous one, so quality is a property of the whole trajectory, not of one output. A classifier emits one label; an LLM emits a path.每個 token 依賴之前所有 token，品質是整條軌跡的性質，而非單一輸出。分類器輸出一個標籤，LLM 輸出的是一條路徑。

03

Trust failures are diverse可信賴性問題多樣

Hallucination, OOD inputs and adversarial perturbations look different but share one need: know when the model is in an unhealthy internal state.幻覺、OOD 輸入、對抗擾動看起來不同，卻共享同一需求：知道模型何時處於不健康的內部狀態。

04

Existing model-based analysis targeted RNN classifiers既有的模型化分析針對 RNN 分類器

DeepStellar, DeepMemory, RNNRepair and friends abstract RNN states into automata. Whether that carries over to Transformers was an open question — LUNA is the exploratory answer.DeepStellar、DeepMemory、RNNRepair 等把 RNN 狀態抽象成自動機；能否延伸到 Transformer 原本未知，LUNA 是探索性的回答。

04

Building the DTMC建構 DTMC

Count abstract transitions, normalize rows, then read off stationary distribution, entropy and sink states計數抽象轉移、列正規化，再讀出穩態分布、熵與匯點狀態

A DTMC is the tuple (S̄, s̄₀, δ̄, P̄). Its transition probability is nothing more than #concrete transitions s̄→s̄′ ÷ #outgoing transitions of s̄. So the model quality depends directly on how many traces you have seen — drag the trace count and watch the estimate converge to the (hidden) truth. Click states in the graph to score a path: for OOD detection, the mean transition probability is the semantic score.DTMC 是元組 (S̄, s̄₀, δ̄, P̄)。轉移機率就是 具體轉移 s̄→s̄′ 的數量 ÷ s̄ 的外向轉移總數，所以模型品質直接取決於看過多少軌跡——拖曳軌跡數，看估計值收斂到（隱藏的）真實值。在圖上依序點選狀態即可為路徑評分：在 OOD 偵測中，平均轉移機率就是語意分數。

05

Semantics binding & token-level detection語意綁定與逐 token 偵測

Every abstract state carries a semantic value; a trace's mean value is its score每個抽象狀態帶有語意值；軌跡的平均值即為分數

Semantics θ says how well the LLM is satisfying a trust property in that state. For hallucination it is the truthfulness of answers that passed through the state (GPT-judge, pass@1, or a GPT-3.5 verdict); for OOD and adversarial inputs it is the transition probability. The abstract semantics θ̄ of a state is the mean over its concrete members. A trace's semantic score is AVG(θ̄(s̄ᵢ)); below a threshold ⇒ abnormal — and the first low state tells you where the model went wrong.語意 θ 表示 LLM 在該狀態下對某項可信賴性的滿足程度。幻覺情境是經過該狀態之答案的真實性（GPT-judge、pass@1 或 GPT-3.5 判定）；OOD 與對抗情境則是轉移機率。狀態的抽象語意 θ̄ 為其具體成員的平均。軌跡的語意分數為 AVG(θ̄(s̄ᵢ))；低於門檻 ⇒ 異常，而第一個低分狀態告訴你模型在哪裡出錯。

06

From scores to ROC AUC從分數到 ROC AUC

The paper's headline metric, reconstructed from its reported mean / median / max以論文報告的平均／中位／最大值重建其主要指標

Detection quality is summarized by ROC AUC: the probability that a random normal sample scores higher than a random abnormal one (0.5 = coin flip). Choose a task and one of its reported AUCs; the widget synthesizes two score populations with exactly that separation (equal-variance Gaussians, AUC = Φ(d′/√2)) so you can see what "0.65" versus "0.90" means in practice.偵測品質以 ROC AUC 概括：隨機一個正常樣本分數高於隨機一個異常樣本的機率（0.5 = 擲硬幣）。選擇任務與其報告的 AUC，元件會合成具有該分離度的兩群分數（等變異數高斯，AUC = Φ(d′/√2)），讓你直觀看到「0.65」與「0.90」的差別。

PAPER
RQ1

Can it tell normal from abnormal?能區分正常與異常嗎？

Yes. Normal-train and normal-test transition distributions coincide; abnormal ones differ (6 of 7 tasks visibly). 20 %–64 % of all 180 models are individually significant (Mann-Whitney U, p < 0.05).可以。正常訓練與測試的轉移分布重合，異常則不同（7 個任務中 6 個明顯）。180 個模型中 20%–64% 各自達顯著（Mann-Whitney U，p < 0.05）。

RQ2

What shapes model quality?什麼影響模型品質？

More PCA components ⇒ better perplexity & coverage but worse sensitivity & SDE. Clustering ⇒ compact and well-covered; grid ⇒ better distribution/transition fidelity. DTMC ≥ HMM on most metrics.PCA 成分越多 ⇒ 困惑度與覆蓋率較佳，但敏感度與 SDE 較差。分群 ⇒ 精簡且覆蓋好；網格 ⇒ 分布／轉移保真較佳。多數指標上 DTMC ≥ HMM。

RQ3

Which metrics predict AUC?哪些指標預測 AUC？

Semantic preciseness (PRE) correlates strongly everywhere (Pearson 0.81–1.00); perplexity is the broadly useful model-wise metric. Others flip sign across tasks — combine several.語意精確度（PRE）處處強相關（Pearson 0.81–1.00）；困惑度是最廣用的模型層級指標。其他指標跨任務正負不一——需組合使用。

Caveat限制

Honest reading誠實解讀

Most mean AUCs sit at 0.55–0.70; the 0.83–0.90 headline numbers are best-of-180 configurations. White-box only, three 7–13B models, and results are configuration-sensitive.多數平均 AUC 落在 0.55–0.70；0.83–0.90 的亮眼數字是 180 種設定中的最佳者。僅限白箱、僅 3 個 7–13B 模型，且結果對設定敏感。
